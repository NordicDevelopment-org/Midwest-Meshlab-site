Known issues worth checking before you flash

Reference noteRead 5 minTags Firmware · Troubleshooting
Where this comes from

Compiled by querying the meshtastic/firmware issue tracker on 29 Aug 2026. Every issue below is linked by number so you can check its current state — several will have been fixed by the time you read this, which is exactly why the method matters more than the list.

Firmware updates are usually uneventful. The exceptions tend to be board-specific rather than general — a build that is fine on one radio can have a real problem on another. Ten minutes on the issue tracker before you flash a node that lives on a rooftop is cheap insurance.

The method

  1. Open the firmware issue tracker.
  2. Search for your board name, not just the symptom — “Heltec V4”, “T-Echo”, “T114”.
  3. Filter to is:open label:bug and sort by newest.
  4. Check whether anything mentions the specific version you are about to install.

That is the whole technique, and it generalises past anything written here.

What was open at the end of August 2026

A snapshot, to show the shape of what turns up. There were around 97 open bug-labelled issues at the time. These are illustrative, not a complete list:

  • A possible receive regression on Heltec V4.3 in 2.7.26 — the kind of report that matters most, because it targets a specific board on a specific build (#11280).
  • Heltec V4 WiFi authentication failing on recent nightly builds, with AUTH_FAIL and handshake timeouts — relevant if your gateway depends on WiFi (#11581).
  • An I2C crash on Heltec T114 that freezes the node without resetting it — the worst failure mode for a remote node, because it stops without rebooting itself (#11301).
  • T-Echo touch button doing nothing (#11610), and display and battery-reporting glitches on ThinkNode M1 (#11578, #11366).
Read the state, not just the title

An issue being open does not mean it is unfixed, and a scary title does not mean it affects you. Check whether it reproduces on your board and your build before letting it change your plans.

Older threads still worth knowing

Some long-standing Bluetooth reports are now closed, but they are still the first thing to search if pairing misbehaves — if you are running older firmware you may be sitting on a bug that has already been fixed upstream:

  • Heltec V3 and LilyGO T3-S3 failing to connect over Bluetooth (#10172).
  • Bluetooth going non-functional after commands are sent over the serial interface (#8747).
  • The BLE pairing PIN barely displaying on Heltec V3 (#8081).

In all three cases the first move is the same: update, then retest.

Habits that make updates boring

  • Update the node on your bench before the one on the roof.
  • Keep one node on a known-good build so you always have a working reference to test against.
  • Note which version each deployed node runs. When something breaks, that list is the first thing you will want.
  • Prefer beta over nightly for anything you cannot easily reach.

If something is already misbehaving, the troubleshooting tool narrows it down before you go bug-hunting.
