Set your region first, or nothing transmits

Reference noteRead 4 minTags Setup · Beginner
Where this comes from

Summarised from the official Meshtastic documentation, linked in full at the bottom. This describes documented behaviour; it is not a write-up of a test we ran.

If you have just flashed a node and nothing you send goes anywhere, it is almost certainly not broken. A freshly flashed node ships with no region configured, and in that state the radio will not transmit at all.

That is deliberate. Which frequencies a LoRa radio may legally use depends on where in the world it is, and the firmware will not guess. Until you tell it, it stays quiet.

Setting it

  1. Connect to the node from the Meshtastic app or the web client.
  2. Open Radio Configuration → LoRa.
  3. Set Region. In the United States that is US.
  4. Let the node reboot, then confirm the setting stuck.

That is the whole fix, and it accounts for a large share of “my new node does not work” reports.

What US actually means

SettingValue
Frequency range902.0 – 928.0 MHz
Power limit30 dBm
Duty cycle100% (no restriction)

This is the 915 MHz ISM band. Operating here does not require an amateur radio licence — standard Meshtastic use is unlicensed operation on a shared band.

The other half of the problem

Region alone is not enough for two nodes to talk. They must also share a modem preset and a channel name and key. A node on a different preset is effectively on its own network even sitting on the same desk — it is the next most common cause of silence after an unset region.

If it still will not transmit

  • Confirm the region really saved — re-read it after the reboot, not before.
  • Check the modem preset matches your local mesh. LONG_FAST is the default.
  • Check the channel name and pre-shared key match on both ends. Sharing the channel QR code is the reliable way to copy them.
  • Make sure an antenna is attached. Transmitting without one can damage the radio.
