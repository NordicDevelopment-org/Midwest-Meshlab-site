Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026
The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no ad trackers, and doesn't sell or share anything you send. The only personal data it collects is what you type into the two forms.

What you send us

Both forms are handled by Netlify Forms, the same service that hosts the site. Submissions are stored in the lab's Netlify account.

FormFieldsUsed for
SubscribeEmail addressSending new builds, videos and event notices
ContactName or callsign, message, optional link, optional emailReading and replying to you; crediting a build you submit for the wall

Nothing you submit is sold, rented, or handed to anyone else. To have a submission deleted or to unsubscribe, use the contact form and say so.

Cookies and local storage

None. The site doesn't set cookies and doesn't write to your browser's storage, which is why there is no cookie banner.

Visit statistics

The lab may count page views with GoatCounter. It records the page, referrer, browser type, screen size and country. It uses no cookies, stores no IP address, and doesn't build a profile of you. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted at all.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.
