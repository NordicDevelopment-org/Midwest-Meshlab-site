01 SITUATION MAP

Minnesota reference map MN-NET

An accurately projected map of Minnesota — Albers Equal Area Conic, drawn from public-domain boundary data, with real city coordinates for orientation. It is a geographic reference, not a telemetry feed: for live node data, use the community maps linked on the right.

Legend

Major city (reference) City (reference) Heard link (when node data is connected)

Projection: Albers Equal Area Conic, standard parallels 44.48°N / 48.40°N. City markers are real coordinates and are not mesh nodes. Node and link layers stay empty until a real data source is connected — see ADMIN.md.

Featured network · Minnesota

MSP Mesh

If you're anywhere near the Twin Cities, this is the network to plug into. MSP Mesh is a decentralized, volunteer-run group building Minnesota's mesh across the Minneapolis–St. Paul metro, Greater Minnesota, and into western Wisconsin — free to use, no license needed. They put nodes on the tall stuff to keep coverage dense and resilient, and their Discord is one of the most welcoming, well-run rooms in the hobby. They deserve more eyes on the work.
